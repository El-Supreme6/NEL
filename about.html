WHAT IS NEL?

The Neo Egoist League is a competitive football league where teams and players compete to prove themselves.

Every match matters. Every performance counts. Players build their reputation through their performances, while teams fight to climb the league and establish themselves as the best.

NEL combines competitive matches, player statistics, awards, transfers and team management into one connected league system.
