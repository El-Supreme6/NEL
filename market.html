MARKET RULES

PLAYER VALUE

Players begin every season with there value from last season. Performance and awards increase their value.

WAGES

Player wages are equal to one 50th of their current value and are paid after each completed matchday.

TRANSFER FEES

Transfer fees are negotiated freely and can be higher, equal to, or lower than a player's value.

TRANSFER SPLIT

50% of the agreed transfer fee goes to the original club and 50% goes to the player.

PLAYER STATUS

Players control their own transfer status: willing, unsure, or unwilling.

FREE AGENTS

Clubs can sign free agents without paying a transfer fee. Any bonuses or additional payments must be agreed upon between the player and the buying club.
